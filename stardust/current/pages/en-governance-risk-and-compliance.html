    Governance, Risk
    and Compliance

    Our mission is to use our wealth of technical and consulting expertise to deliver tailored and pragmatic solutions, building meaningful and long-term relationships with our clients, adapting to the industry’s ever-changing needs.

    Areas of expertise

    Risk, Finance & Compliance

    We help to address the implications of risk, finance & compliance related challenges. We focus on improving business insight and reducing costs, with experience in programme and transformation delivery.

    Quantitative Risk Management

    We support our clients across the model lifecycle, from development to measurement and validation. Our teams specialise in relevant model types – including risk, capital, liquidity, pricing, and stress testing models.

    Data Management and Implementations

    We help our clients to design, develop and implement innovative and efficient technology enabled solutions in areas of data management and governance, process automation and regulatory reporting.

    Regulatory Engagement

    We support our clients in managing their regulatory relationships and in obtaining regulatory permissions and authorisations. We help them navigate the complex regulatory landscape to achieve optimal outcomes.
