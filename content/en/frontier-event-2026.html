Italian Premiere: The First Event Dedicated to Microsoft 365 E7

Reply and Microsoft are bringing a new vision for enterprise AI to the stage with the introduction of Microsoft 365 E7, the Frontier Suite designed to unify advanced productivity, digital identity, security, and agent governance within a single integrated platform.

Hosted at Microsoft’s Envisioning Theater in Milan on September 22, 2026, this exclusive event offers a unique opportunity to discover how organizations can prepare for the next frontier of artificial intelligence and unlock the full potential of AI at scale.

AI is no longer a side project

Organizations across every industry are looking to embed AI into everyday work at scale. To support this transformation, Microsoft introduces its Frontier Suite.

Microsoft 365 E7 marks a significant evolution in enterprise technology strategy, placing AI at the core of the platform rather than treating it as an optional add-on.

E7 – The Ultimate Journey explores this transformation through Reply’s experience supporting organizations that are integrating people and AI agents to accelerate productivity, innovation, and business growth.

Together, we will explore the most impactful opportunities to reshape the workplace and unlock new sources of value.

The event delivers an immersive Microsoft 365 E7 experience, showcasing a unified approach that brings together Microsoft 365 E5, Copilot, Entra Suite, and Agent 365 to provide the highest levels of intelligence, security, governance, and trust.

Through real-world scenarios, use cases, live demonstrations, and discussions with Microsoft and Reply experts, attendees will discover how to build a scalable, governed AI strategy capable of delivering measurable value across the entire organization.
