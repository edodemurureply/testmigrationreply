Environment

Embracing sustainable and green principles is not just a trend, it is a strategic decision that brings many advantages. We have set out guidelines that can positively affect the company’s impact on the environment and that can be applied in all our premises worldwide.

We are also committed to involving our people in sustainability initiatives aimed at increasing awareness of sustainable themes, and collecting brilliant ideas for internal projects on sustainability.

TIME World's Best Companies in Sustainable Growth 2026


Reply has been named by TIME and Statista as one of the “World’s Best Companies in Sustainable Growth 2026,” recognizing companies that combine growth and financial strength with a lower environmental impact.

ROADMAP

Waste management

Reply is committed, whenever possible, to reusing and recycling electronic waste and to eliminating the use of single-use plastics.

Discover more

Zero waste

We are committed to achieving zero-waste status by 2030. Reply will donate, reuse or recycle 100% of e-waste such as computers and servers.

Donations

We are committed to donating electronic equipment, both to our employees and charities.

Single-use plastic

We have set a goal to eliminate single-use plastic in all our locations, consistent with the European Single Use Plastic (SUP) Directive.

We strictly consider environmental factors when procuring goods and services, promoting suppliers who have a proven track record in sustainability in their operations, which gives them a competitive advantage.
