In this episode, we talk to Joseph B Crosswhite, a pioneer and innovator in using VR and metaverse technologies for education in emerging markets. We ask him how he is solving the challenge of limited access to technology in these regions and how he plans to scale his impact. We also learn about his current focus markets and products, and how he leverages VR to create interactive and personalized learning experiences. Finally, we hear about his study with USAID on the effectiveness of VR training and some of the examples he has seen.

Tune in to this inspiring conversation and discover how VR and metaverse can transform education for millions of learners!
