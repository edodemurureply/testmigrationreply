In this episode, we talk to Steve Suarez, Global Head of Innovation, Global Functions at HSBC and a visionary leader and expert in the fields of metaverse, quantum computing and finance. We ask him some intriguing questions about his views on the metaverse. We also explore the potential and opportunities of the metaverse for finance, education and more. We discuss some of the challenges that the metaverse is facing for broader adoption in general and in particular for the financial industry. Finally, we look into the crystal ball and ask him about his vision for the metaverse in a few years with finance and how quantum computing relates to it.
