Why ethical AI matters
to Reply

As global regulations reshape the digital landscape, Ethical AI is no longer just a compliance checkbox - it is the foundation of digital trust. For Reply, robust AI governance and proactive risk management are essential to protecting our clients and society.

We believe that innovation must always be paired with accountability. This commitment is deeply integrated into our broader Ethical AI initiatives and Corporate ESG strategy.

The ethical AI user group

To turn our vision into reality, we have established the Ethical AI User Group - an international community of responsible leaders from top-tier companies and universities.

Our mission & purpose

Our mission is to share knowledge, experience, and questions on Ethical AI best practices to achieve continuous improvement based on collective intelligence. By collaborating, we enable our members to profit from each other's insights, defining the right actions tailored to specific countries, industries, and use cases. We provide the orientation and guidelines necessary to drive AI development in a beneficial, ethical, and legally compliant direction.

Who should join?

We are building a highly curated, cross-functional network. Membership is free of charge and open to Reply customers and academic partners.

You will be joining digital, compliance, and HR leaders from global organizations and leading academic institutions. Currently the user group consists of C-Level, Directors, and Heads of Departments appointed by their organizations, specifically from:

Digital & IT

CIOs, CISOs, CTOs

Governance

Compliance and Legal Experts

Culture & people

HR Leaders

Ecosystem

Politicians, Authorities, and University Researchers across the EU, UK, and US.

The industry pulse:
How we collaborate

The user group’s member organisations identified their biggest AI governance challenges as measuring ethical outcomes and navigating unclear regulatory requirements. While principles like Transparency, Security, and Privacy are top priorities, moving from theory to practice remains the hurdle. This User Group exists to bridge that exact gap.

Governance & how we work:

We are an active, results-driven community. Our activities and decision frameworks are structured to provide maximum value:

Quarterly meetings

Fixed sessions (remote and in-person) with the possibility for deep-dives in between.

Member-driven agenda

Priority topics are determined by the user group members themselves.

Collaborative decision framework

The User Group operates on a consensus-driven model, guided by our internal Ethical AI Innovation Team, ensuring all deliverables are peer-reviewed and practically applicable.

Dedicated workstream 1

Compliance (Regulatory Strategy, EU AI Act, Standards, Risk Management) & Strategy (Corporate alignment, Stewardship, Third-party/Supply Chain AI risk).

Dedicated workstream 2

Tools (Frameworks, Metrics, Model Behaviour, Guardrails).

Dedicated workstream 3

Training & Awareness (Building internal culture and digital acculturation).

Accompanying topics

AI Sovereignty and Business Resilience.

Our code of conduct

To maintain the high quality of our network, we operate under strict shared values:

A safe space for data

Members must anonymize all examples, logs, and screenshots before sharing. We use secure, dedicated platforms for collaboration.

Education &
compliance first

Focus on sharing informative, regulatory, and technical content.

Non-commercial

This is a dedicated space for peer-to-peer knowledge sharing and problem-solving, free from sales pitches or promotional content.

Inclusive collaboration

We respect differing opinions and promote a positive, cross-industry environment.
